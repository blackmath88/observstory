Observatory Tortoise

The owner's painted north star, turned into pixel art at 320×179 with one shared 64-colour palette, cut into layers and brought to life. The telescope now lives in the dome slit. Resting and up are separate painted frames; the dome opens as a pixel reveal that spreads out from the slit. All project data shown is synthetic.

Resting
Nothing needs a conversation. Dome closed, sleepy eyes behind the portholes, slow breathing; now and then it peeks.

An overlap or a wait exists. The dome opens, the telescope comes out, the eyes glow, the lantern burns.
Travelling
Replay is playing. Dome open, a slow plod, shooting stars overhead. A real walk needs leg frames (see below).

Built from these frames

Pixelated from the owner's Nano Banana images, same palette, no hand retouching yet. The cut-outs come from the flat-background frame and are what the product uses.
