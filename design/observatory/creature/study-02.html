Walking Observatory II

This time the observatory is the whole creature. The patched dome and its stone drum are the body, the telescope is the snout, and the portholes are its eyes. The tinkering is all instruments: armillary or anemometer on the crown, a turret dome, a sextant arm, a heliostat mirror, ladders, a balcony walkway and ivy. The palette is cooler: verdigris, slate and stone, with brass only as accents. Tinkered, wholesome, unhurried. Every pixel is drawn by code from a seed. All project data mentioned here is synthetic.

Decisions for you

Locomotion

Tortoise (sturdy, closest to round 3), stilt-walker (most "walking house"), or drifter (hovers; most magical, least grounded).

Fidelity

This study draws at 160×120 art pixels with shading and dithering. Higher reads richer but animates slower to hand-tune; lower returns to the round-3 chunkiness.

Face

Two porthole windows read as eyes and the telescope as a snout. Keep it subtle (current), add a brow (a cornice over the portholes), or let only one porthole glow.

Material

Verdigris dome on cool stone with slate patches (current), a darker slate dome, or a pale whitewashed drum for more contrast.
