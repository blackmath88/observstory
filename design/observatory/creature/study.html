Walking Observatory

An old, patched-together observatory that is quietly alive. It lies down as a building when nothing needs a conversation, stands when something does, and travels when you replay history. Tinkered, wholesome, unhurried. Every pixel below is drawn by code from a seed, so any part can animate. All project data mentioned here is synthetic.

Decisions for you

Locomotion

Tortoise (sturdy, closest to round 3), stilt-walker (most "walking house"), or drifter (hovers; most magical, least grounded).

Fidelity

This study draws at 160×120 art pixels with shading and dithering. Higher reads richer but animates slower to hand-tune; lower returns to the round-3 chunkiness.

Face

The hull has a porthole eye and a grille that can read as a mouth. Keep it subtle (current), stronger, or remove the grille entirely.

Material

Rusted iron hull with a verdigris copper dome (current), all-copper, or slate-grey iron like an old workshop.
