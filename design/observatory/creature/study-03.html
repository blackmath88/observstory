Brutalist Observatory Creature

A gentle salamander-tortoise whose back is a modern observatory cast in concrete. Two curved concrete shells part like wing cases to reveal a pale lattice telescope; a cantilevered collar carries a ribbon of lit windows; porthole eyes have wooden shutters for eyelids. Copper only in the seams. Drawn by code at 256×160 pixels so every part can move. All project data shown is synthetic.

In the product shell

Brutalism in the UI: recessed slabs, a cantilevered header with tabs cut into it, 2px joints, one chamfer, and the ribbon-window lane strip. Lit means work is in flight; an orange frame means it needs a conversation; every slot says so in words.
